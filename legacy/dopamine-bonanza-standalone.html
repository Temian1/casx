Paytable & Rules

Pays anywhere. There are no paylines. Land 8 or more of the same symbol anywhere on the 6×5 grid and it pays — position is irrelevant. Payouts above are multiples of your total bet, split into three count bands: 8–9, 10–11, and 12+.

Tumble. Every winning symbol is removed, everything above it drops down, and fresh symbols fall in from the top. Tumbles repeat for free until no new win forms, and all wins in the sequence add up.

Free spins. Land 4 or more dopamine molecules (scatter) anywhere to win 10 free spins. Landing 3+ scatters during the feature retriggers +5 spins.

Multiplier orbs. During free spins only, orbs worth 2× to 100× drop onto the grid. They never pay by themselves and survive every tumble — when the sequence ends, all orb values on screen are added together and applied to that spin's whole win.

Double Chance raises your stake by 25% and doubles the scatter rate. Buy Free Spins costs 100× bet; Super Free Spins costs 500× and doubles both the frequency and the richness of the orbs.
